I am a software engineer in Houston. I write long-form explanations of mathematics, derivations included, and build open-source tools for human languages. Recent work includes six-volume series on why no formula in radicals solves the quintic and on how a sphere turns inside out, and glottosphere.com, a dictionary in which every word of a definition is a link.

I studied computer science at the University of Pennsylvania, graduating in 2020 with a thesis on obfuscation as a privacy mechanism. Many of the volumes carry interactive figures you can drag and step through.

Interests

  • Mathematics from first principles: multi-volume series that prove one theorem or derive one formula end to end, namely Abel–Ruffini, Sphere Eversion, On Multiplication, Primitive Sets, Mean-Reverting VIX and Black-Scholes-Merton, plus standalone explainers such as Grover's Search.
  • Human languages: glottosphere.com, a multilingual dictionary; 1wg.ai, world news gathered across languages; Mr. Worldwide, README Rosetta, polyglot-poster and vulgultra, a Romance conlang.
  • Software and numerics: intersekt, big-integer multiplication in WebAssembly; vakyume, textbook equations turned into verified Python and C++ solvers; rafaga, VIX models in Julia; and the developer tools ghee and tatuagem.
