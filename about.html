Houstonian software engineer with a passion for mathematics and human languages.

I work on commodity trading systems. Before that I built telemetry for connected farm equipment and analytics for cybersecurity training.

The series cover why the quintic has no formula in radicals, how a sphere turns inside out, how fast two integers multiply, and how volatility is priced.

The tools render a word in eighty languages, translate a project’s documentation with a local model, and turn a textbook into a six-language poster.

I hold a bachelor’s in Computer Science from the University of Pennsylvania, where I wrote a thesis on obfuscation as a privacy mechanism.
