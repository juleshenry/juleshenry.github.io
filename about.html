Software engineer in Houston. I write about mathematics and build tools for languages.

I’m Julian Henry. I work on commodity trading systems, and before that I built telemetry for connected farm equipment and analytics for cybersecurity training.

This site holds two kinds of work. The first is a set of multi-volume explanations: why the quintic has no formula in radicals, how a sphere turns inside out, how fast two integers can be multiplied, and how volatility is priced. Each one starts from definitions and keeps the derivations in.

The second is open-source software, much of it about language. One tool renders a word in eighty languages, another translates a project’s documentation with a local model, and a third turns a textbook into a six-language poster.

I studied computer science at the University of Pennsylvania, where I wrote a thesis on obfuscation as a privacy mechanism, and I hold the DELE B2 diploma in Spanish.
